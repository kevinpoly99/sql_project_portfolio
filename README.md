# Findings
### 💰 1. Top-Paying Business Analyst Roles
#### [Go to Query](/project_sql/1_Top_paying_jobs.sql)


Certain specific roles in Business Analyst's salary ranges from **$100,000 to $162,500** among the top results.

- 🏆 **Highest salary:** $162,500 at CyberCoders.
- 📊 **Common roles:** Business Intelligence Analyst and Business Data Analyst.
- 💡 **Key insight:** Specialized and senior-level analyst positions can offer strong earning potential, particularly when combined with technical expertise.


### 🛠️ 2. Most In-Demand Skills 
#### [Go to Query](/project_sql/3_Top_skills.sql)

 **SQL, Power BI, Excel, and Tableau** are among the most frequently requested skills across Business Analyst postings.

- 🥇 **SQL:** 1,636 times mentioned for Business Analyst roles.
- 📊 **Power BI:** 1,105 times mentioned for Business Analyst roles.
- 📈 **Excel:** 1,031 times mentioned for Business Analyst roles.

💡 **Key insight:** SQL proficiency, spreadsheet analysis, and data visualization tools are valuable skills for aspiring business analysts.

### 💵 3. Average Salary associated with skills
#### [Go to Query](/project_sql/4_Skills_to_learn.sql)

Here I look for average annual salaries for each skill in business analyst roles. SQL had the highest demand in the results, with **312 mentions** and an average salary of approximately **$95,292**. Python showed an average salary of **$104,277**, while Tableau averaged **$98,794**.

💡 **Key insight:** Frequently requested Business Analyst skills such as SQL, Excel, and Tableau offer a strong foundation, while specialized technical skills offers higher average salaries. However, salary differences can also reflect job seniority and role requirements.
